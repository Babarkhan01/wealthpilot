LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026 — Last updated: June 15, 2026

🔒 Short version: WealthPilot collects only your name and email to provide access. We never store your clients' financial data. We never sell your information. Ever.

1. Who we are

WealthPilot is an AI-powered tax and retirement research tool for fee-only Certified Financial Planners (CFPs) in the United States. WealthPilot is an independent, self-funded product with no venture capital or third-party investors. Contact: support@wealthpilot.co

2. What data we collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Name and email address — collected at signup, used to manage your subscription and send updates.
  • Consent confirmation — whether you agreed to receive product updates.
  • Usage counters — query count per day, stored in your browser only. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Session conversation history — stored in browser memory only. Cleared when you close or refresh the page.

We do not collect your clients' names, financial information, Social Security numbers, account numbers, or any personally identifiable information about any third party.

3. How we use your data

  • To grant and manage your access to WealthPilot
  • To send product updates and important notices (if you consented)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the product based on aggregated, anonymised patterns

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

4. AI processing — Anthropic Claude

WealthPilot uses the Claude API by Anthropic, PBC. When you submit a query, it is sent to Anthropic's API for processing under their Privacy Policy. Do not include client names, Social Security numbers, account numbers, or any personally identifiable client information in queries. Use anonymised scenarios (e.g., "a 62-year-old client with $800k in a traditional IRA").

5. Data storage and retention

  • Conversation history: browser memory only, cleared on page close.
  • Name and email: stored in your browser localStorage and our email notification system (EmailJS) for access management only.
  • Usage counters: browser localStorage only.
  • We do not operate a server-side database of your conversations.
  • Request deletion: email support@wealthpilot.co — we action within 30 days.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with: EmailJS (email notifications), Anthropic (AI processing), and Cloudflare (hosting). Each operates under their own privacy policy. We may also disclose data if required by law.

7. Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of marketing at any time by emailing us.
  • Non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights (CCPA).

California residents: WealthPilot has not sold personal information to any third party as defined under the CCPA.

To exercise any right: support@wealthpilot.co. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

WealthPilot uses browser localStorage only — not cookies. We do not use advertising pixels or third-party behavioral analytics.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. API keys are stored in Cloudflare Worker environment variables and never exposed in client-side code. We apply input sanitisation and rate limiting. Do not submit highly sensitive personal data through the chat interface.

10. Children's privacy

WealthPilot is a professional tool for licensed financial advisors. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Contact us to request deletion of any minor's data.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date when this policy changes. Continued use constitutes acceptance. We will notify users by email of significant changes.

12. Contact

Email: support@wealthpilot.co — Response within 2 business days.
